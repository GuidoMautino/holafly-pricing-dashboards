3 · The rule, today and proposed

Today in Plans

One euro-shaped rule for all 25 currencies. Under 10 it ends in .49, between 10 and 999 it drops to the integer minus .05, above 1,000 it drops to the ten plus 9.90. The same ending everywhere, regardless of the market. That is how a plan ends up at £38.95 while the eSIM catalogue works in whole pounds in 98.7% of its prices.

Proposed

One ending per currency, built on each market's convention. Euro and dollar end in .90 or .50, pound and Pacific dollars go to whole units, Gulf plus Poland and Israel to integers ending in 4, 7 or 9, Chile, Colombia and Korea to X999 and X900. It is the table already running across the eSIM catalogue, validated price by price against production.
